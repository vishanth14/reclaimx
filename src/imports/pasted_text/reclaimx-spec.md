============================================================

NOT a traditional marketing landing page.

"I can report something I lost."

"I can report something I found."

"ReclaimX connects them."

"Ownership is verified privately."

SMART RECOVERY NETWORK

Lost isn't the end.

ReclaimX connects lost things with the people who found them — then verifies ownership before they change hands.

LOST → MATCHED → VERIFIED → RECLAIMED

============================================================
OWNERSHIP VERIFICATION
============================================================

This is the signature product screen.

Headline:

Prove it's yours.

Supporting text:

"Answer using what you remember. Private evidence submitted by the finder is never revealed."

Show:

PRIVATE VERIFICATION

01 / 03

Questions such as:

"What was attached to the backpack?"

"Where was the distinctive damage?"

"What was inside the backpack?"

Inputs should feel premium and calm.

Do NOT reveal whether individual answers are correct while typing.

Only evaluate on final submission.

============================================================
VERIFICATION RESULT
============================================================

Successful state:

OWNERSHIP VERIFIED

Show evidence categories:

Item characteristics
Private details
Time consistency
Location consistency

Copy:

"Ownership evidence matched the verification criteria."

Primary:

Request Handover

Never say:

"AI proved ownership."

Never say:

"100% verified by AI."

============================================================
FAILED VERIFICATION
============================================================

Use:

"Verification needs more evidence."

Supporting:

"Some of the information did not match the verification criteria."

Actions:

Try Again
Cancel Claim

Do not accuse the claimant of fraud.
